The first week is all about warmth, milk, and sleep. Coco took to motherhood immediately — she has
barely left the whelping box, and every puppy latched on and started gaining.

We weigh all nine daily and log it, which is the quickest way to catch a puppy who needs a little
extra help at the milk bar. Around day three we also began **ENS** (early neurological stimulation)
and **ESI** (early scent introduction) — a few seconds of gentle handling and a new scent for each
puppy, once a day. It is a small, well-studied routine that helps build resilience and a steadier
nervous system later in life.
