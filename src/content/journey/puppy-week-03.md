Eyes and ears are working, and the whole litter has switched from squeaking blobs to genuinely
curious little dogs. This is when the world starts arriving on purpose rather than by accident.

We began layering in **socialization sounds** — the vacuum, the doorbell, a thunderstorm recording,
the dishwasher — at a low volume while they nurse and nap, so the noises of a normal house register
as ordinary rather than alarming. Textured surfaces went into the pen too: crinkly fabric, a rubber
mat, a wobble board. Nine sets of paws figuring out that the floor can feel like more than one thing.
