This is the big one for socialization, and we have been deliberate about it. The puppies have now met
a genuine variety of people — toddlers, men in hats, and their great-grandma among them. Sounds
obvious, but it matters: a puppy who has only met one kind of person can find every other kind
startling. Nine puppies who think new people are wonderful is the goal.

**Potty training has begun.** At this age they instinctively want to leave the sleeping area to go,
so we have given them somewhere obvious to do it. It gets a head start on the habit long before
they go home.

Enrichment continues daily — new textures, new noises, new small challenges. They are also getting
properly good at kibble.
