Teeth arrived, and Coco has opinions about that. Right on schedule, we introduced **puppy mush** — a
quality puppy kibble soaked soft and warm. The first meal is more of a face wash than a meal, and it
is one of the great comedies of raising a litter.

Toys came out this week as well: things that squeak, things that roll, things that are just about
chewable. They are still nursing, but the shift toward solid food is properly underway.
